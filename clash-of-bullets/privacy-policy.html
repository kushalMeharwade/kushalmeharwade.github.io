Clash of Bullets · Android · Ages 13+

Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026 · Developer: Kushal Meharwade, operating as Kushal’s GameZone

Pre-release policy. Clash of Bullets is in development. This policy describes the current implementation. Google test ads are enabled; production ad IDs, AdMob dashboard setup and native-device validation are not finalized. We will review this policy against the release build before public release.

This policy explains how Clash of Bullets for Android and this developer website handle information. Contact work.kushalmeharwade@gmail.com for privacy questions.

1. Information stored on your device

The game uses Unity PlayerPrefs to save your chosen player name, character customization, gameplay and audio preferences, and player statistics such as matches, wins, kills and damage. It also generates and saves a random LAN session identifier so a host can recognize a reconnecting player. These local settings and statistics are not uploaded to a developer-operated cloud-save service.

The current game does not require a developer-operated account, email address or password to play. A chosen player name is visible to other participants in multiplayer; use a nickname rather than personal or sensitive information.

2. LAN multiplayer and information shared with players

LAN discovery and multiplayer exchange network traffic with devices on your local network. Your device’s local network address is used to connect. The game sends your chosen name, character appearance and LAN session identifier to the match host. Names, appearances, gameplay state and match scores are shared with participating devices as needed to run the match and display results.

The host temporarily keeps reconnect information, including match statistics, for a rejoin window of up to 120 seconds; session cleanup can remove it earlier. Match results may remain in memory until cleared or the app closes. The current implementation does not provide developer-operated public matchmaking, player accounts or a cloud leaderboard. We cannot control another participant’s screenshots or copies of information they can see.

3. Google advertising and consent services

The app integrates Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). Test ads still involve SDK network requests and can process device information. Depending on SDK configuration, consent, region and device settings, Google advertising services process data such as IP addresses (which may indicate approximate location), app and ad interactions, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers. Google describes advertising, analytics and fraud prevention as processing purposes. Advertising and consent services may store information on the device.

The current placement is an interstitial at an eligible completed-match break: after returning to the main menu or after four seconds on the results screen. Rewarded ads are disabled in the current configuration. We have not added a separate developer-operated analytics service in the game’s custom code; this does not mean advertising services perform no analytics.

Google’s processing and retention are described in Google’s Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from partner apps, and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures.

4. Your advertising and privacy choices

The game updates consent information through Google UMP from the main menu. When required by the applicable configuration and region, it presents a consent message before requesting ads. You can review or change advertising privacy choices through Settings → AD PRIVACY when Google UMP requires that control. Available consent and opt-out choices depend on region and configuration. Declining consent does not necessarily disable every ad; Google determines which ad requests are permitted.

Android also provides advertising identifier controls where available. We do not currently provide an age-verification screen or a separate ad-free mode for teenagers. Production consent messaging and device behavior must be validated before live advertising is enabled.

5. Intended audience

Clash of Bullets is intended for teens and adults aged 13 and above and is not directed to children under 13. The current game does not collect a date of birth or verify age. An intended audience of 13+ is not a claim that all players can independently consent to advertising in every region. If you are a parent or guardian with a privacy concern, contact us using the email below and avoid sending unnecessary personal details about a child.

6. Deletion, retention and security

You can remove local player settings, statistics and the saved LAN identifier by clearing the game’s storage in Android settings. This resets your local profile and preferences. Android backup and restore settings may affect whether data is restored. Removing your local data does not remove copies already seen or retained by other players or information independently processed by Google.

Local data remains until removed or replaced through app or device controls. LAN session state is used for the match and reconnect behavior described above. Local multiplayer traffic should not be assumed to provide end-to-end encryption; play on a network you trust. Google documents encryption in transit for information collected by its Mobile Ads SDK. No storage or network connection can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Support messages and privacy requests

If you email us, your email provider and ours process your address, message and attachments. We use correspondence to answer questions, investigate problems and address privacy requests. We retain it as needed for those purposes and applicable obligations. You may request access, correction or deletion of correspondence by contacting us. Information may need to be retained to meet obligations or resolve disputes. Use Google’s privacy controls for information Google holds independently.

8. This website

This policy page contains no advertising scripts, tracking pixels, analytics scripts, forms or account sign-in. It does not set its own cookies. GitHub Pages hosts the website and processes visitor information, including IP addresses, for hosting and security under GitHub’s Privacy Statement. External links open services with their own privacy practices.
