Hopper Haulers

Privacy notice

Prepared: 14 October 2026 · Developer: Kushal Meharwade, operating as Kushal’s GameZone

Pre-release notice. Hopper Haulers is in development and is intended for teens and adults aged 13 and above. Current development configuration uses Google test ads. Teen/under-age-of-consent protections, age handling and consent configuration are not yet finalized. This notice describes the current development implementation and must be reviewed and updated against the final app before its public release.

This notice explains how information is handled by Hopper Haulers for Android and this studio website. For privacy questions, email work.kushalmeharwade@gmail.com.

1. Information saved on your device

The game saves mission stars, unlocked missions, coin balance, your Score Attack best score, session count, sound and haptic preferences, and a record used to prevent duplicate ad rewards. The game’s code stores these using Unity’s local PlayerPrefs storage, including a local backup of progress. It does not upload these game saves to a developer-operated cloud service.

The current game has no developer-operated player accounts, chat, public player profiles, cloud saves or global leaderboards. It does not ask you to enter your name, email address or date of birth to play.

2. Google advertising and privacy services

The app integrates Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). Current development builds are configured to request sample ads. Test ads do not mean the SDK makes no network requests or processes no device information.

Depending on SDK configuration, consent, region and device settings, Google’s advertising services may collect and share information such as IP address (which can indicate approximate location), app/ad interactions, diagnostic information and device or account identifiers. Google describes advertising, analytics and fraud prevention among its processing purposes. SDK and consent services may store information on your device.

The current implementation includes optional rewarded ads for a double-coins bonus and interstitial ads at eligible results transitions. Publisher ad units remain disabled in the development configuration. We have not implemented an additional developer-operated analytics service in the game’s custom code.

For Google’s practices and controls, see Google’s Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from partner apps, and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures. Those services have their own processing and retention practices.

3. Intended audience and younger users

Hopper Haulers is intended for teens and adults aged 13 and above; it is not designed or marketed specifically to children under 13. This intended audience is not an age-verification mechanism. The current development build does not verify a player's age or implement an age-based advertising route.

Teen and under-age-of-consent protections remain relevant because privacy rules and age thresholds vary by region. Age handling, appropriate ad treatment and consent flows must be completed and verified before live ads are enabled. We do not request personal information through an in-game account or form.

A parent or guardian with a privacy concern can contact work.kushalmeharwade@gmail.com. Please do not include a child’s personal details unless needed to explain the issue. This notice will be updated when the final audience protections are implemented.

4. Your controls and deleting local data

  • Sound and haptic settings can be changed in the game.
  • The app displays a Privacy Choices control when Google UMP indicates that privacy options are required. Available choices depend on the applicable region and configuration.
  • You can clear the app’s storage through Android settings to remove local game progress and preferences. This resets your progress. Android backup/restore settings may affect whether app data is restored later.
  • Android provides controls for advertising identifiers where available. Google provides further controls for information it processes.

5. Support messages

If you email the studio, the email service processes your email address, message and any attachments you send. We use this correspondence to respond to your enquiry, investigate issues and address privacy requests. We retain correspondence as needed for those purposes and applicable obligations. You may request deletion of correspondence by emailing us; some information may need to be retained to meet legal obligations or resolve disputes. We cannot directly delete information held independently by Google; use its privacy controls for those requests.

6. Security and retention

Local game saves remain on your device until removed or replaced through device/app controls. The game does not operate a server-side backup of these saves. We do not promise that local storage or email is perfectly secure. Google documents encryption in transit for data collected by its Mobile Ads SDK; its policies govern its retention. We will update this notice if additional services or data uses are introduced.

7. This website and hosting

The supplied website pages do not include advertising scripts, tracking pixels, analytics scripts, contact forms or account sign-in. They do not set their own cookies. External links and email links open third-party services whose policies apply. If published on GitHub Pages, GitHub processes visitors’ IP addresses for hosting security, as described in GitHub’s hosting documentation and GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

8. Changes and contact
